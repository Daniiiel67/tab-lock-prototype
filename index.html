Task: You're in a meeting with your project partner. Look through your slides and notes without losing the meeting tab. When you're done, close the meeting.

Keyboard shortcuts:
  • ← → switch tabs
  • Shift + L lock / unlock the current tab
  • Shift + W close the current tab

You can also right-click a tab.
